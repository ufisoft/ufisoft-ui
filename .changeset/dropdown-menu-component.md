Add `DropdownMenu` with `DropdownMenuItem`, `DropdownMenuSeparator` and `DropdownMenuLabel`: a list of actions opened from a trigger, built on Radix DropdownMenu (new dependency `@radix-ui/react-dropdown-menu`). Items support `tone="danger"`, `disabled` and `asChild` for links. Rendered next to its trigger, so it also works inside `Modal`.
