Add `DateRangePicker`: start and end date fields, typed or picked from one range calendar, with `min`/`max`, `sm`/`md`/`lg` sizes, `FormField` support (the field's label names the group), translatable labels and format through `locale`, and hidden `yyyy-MM-dd` inputs via `startName`/`endName`. Exports the `DateRange` type. `DatePicker` behaviour is unchanged; its docs now point to `DateRangePicker` for ranges.
